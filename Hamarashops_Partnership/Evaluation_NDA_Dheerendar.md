# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AND EVALUATION AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure and Evaluation Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of **August 20, 2026** (the "Effective Date"), by and between:

**Disclosing Party:** Robert McCarthy / Uncle Robert Consulting LLC ("AgentLab") **Receiving Party:** Virtusa / Dheerendar Srivastav

Collectively referred to as the "Parties."

## 1. Purpose
The Parties intend to participate in discussions and technical demonstrations regarding a potential business partnership, consulting arrangement, or white-labeling agreement (the "Purpose"). In connection with the Purpose, AgentLab will disclose certain proprietary and confidential information.

## 2. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means any and all technical and non-technical information provided by AgentLab, whether disclosed orally, visually, or in writing. This explicitly includes, but is not limited to:
* The architecture, logic, and design of the **"Founder Signal System"** and **"Pulse Social"**.
* Visual or operational demonstrations of **n8n automated workflows**, canvases, and node routing.
* Prompt engineering techniques, CRM integration strategies (including HubSpot pipelines), and backend schemas.
* Business models, pricing strategies, and go-to-market plans.

## 3. Obligations of the Receiving Party
The Receiving Party agrees to:
a) Hold the Confidential Information in strict confidence.
b) Use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose of evaluating a partnership with AgentLab.
c) Not disclose the Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent.
d) Protect the Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information.

## 4. Non-Circumvention & No Reverse Engineering
The Receiving Party agrees that they will not use the Confidential Information to reverse-engineer, replicate, or independently develop a competing product or service (including n8n automation flows and content-to-CRM pipelines) that mirrors the functionality demonstrated by AgentLab.

## 5. Term
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall remain in effect for a period of two (2) years. The obligations of confidentiality shall survive the termination of this Agreement for an additional three (3) years.

## 6. No License
Nothing in this Agreement is intended to grant any rights or licenses under any intellectual property rights of AgentLab, except the limited right to review the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose.
