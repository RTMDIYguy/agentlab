# Tactix Outreach SDR SOP

## Purpose

This document defines the workflow and incentive structure for Kausar, Jessie Fisher (or any other Upwork Freelancer / agency partner via Tactix) to manage the CRM-lite and execute manual outreach for URC.

The goal is to shift lead generation and event invitations from a founder-led task to an SDR-led task, allowing Robert to focus purely on fulfillment, advisory, and closing qualified leads.

## Role & Responsibilities

- **Monitor the CRM:** Keep the Google Sheet updated daily with new engagement.
- **Connection Requests:** Send connection requests on LinkedIn to targeted ICP profiles and people engaging with URC content.
- **Initial Outreach:** Send the first direct message offering **The Agentic OS Blueprint** (Gumroad link) or inviting connections to the Founder Roundtable.
- **Follow-up:** Execute the follow-up sequences in the CRM based on the follow-up dates.

## Incentive Structure

Because URC operates as a bootstrapped startup, we use a 100% performance-based compensation model. There is no hourly base pay, but we offer a much larger share of the upside for successful execution.

### Performance Bounty (Conversion Only)

- **Front-End Bounty:** $150 per Qualified Lead that actually attends a Founder Roundtable OR shows up to a Discovery Call.
- **Back-End Commission:** 20% of the first month's revenue for any consulting deal or continuity offer closed from a lead you generated.
- **Requirement:** The lead must actually show up to the event or call to qualify for the front-end bounty. You are responsible for ensuring they are reminded and engaged leading up to the event.

## The Handoff (When Does Kausar Stop?)

Kausar's job is complete on a specific lead when one of two things happens:

1. **The lead registers for an event / books a call:** Kausar updates the Google Sheet status to "Booked" or "Registered", notifies Robert, and steps away. Robert takes over the relationship from there.
2. **The lead opts out / says no:** Kausar updates the Google Sheet to "Dead/Not Interested" and stops follow-up.

## Daily Workflow Checklist

1. **Check the Google Sheet:** Open the CRM-lite and filter for leads with today's date in the "Follow-up Date" column.
2. **Execute Follow-ups:** Send the appropriate scripted follow-up message via LinkedIn DM or Email.
3. **Log Activity:** Update the status and next follow-up date in the CRM.
4. **Source New Leads:** Review engagement on Robert's recent LinkedIn posts. Send connection requests to 2nd/3rd degree connections who liked or commented.
5. **Send Invites:** For new connections, send the standard invitation script to the next Founder Roundtable.
6. **Report:** Send a brief end-of-day message to Robert (via Slack/Upwork) summarizing: New connections made, messages sent, and any meetings booked.
