# URC CRM-Lite & Book Funnel

## 1. The Book Funnel
**Front-End Promise:** Simplify the tools founders already have.
**Back-End Strategy:** Migrate the right businesses into Agentic OS.

**The Book CTA Flow:**
1. **Content:** LinkedIn & Instagram posts (derived from book ideas and real build logs).
2. **Book Read:** Reader finds value in the transparency and practical operating architectures.
3. **Primary CTA:** Join a Free Founder Roundtable / Diagnostic Session.
4. **Follow-up:** Nurture sequence via CRM-lite leading to a continuity offer, consulting sprint, or workshop.
5. **Final Path:** Migration into Agentic OS.

### Pipeline Stages
- **Lead Captured**: Initial contact made via book CTA or LinkedIn outreach.
- **Diagnostic Booked**: Scheduled for roundtable or 1:1 diagnostic.
- **Proposal Sent**: Post-diagnostic action plan delivered.
- **Continuity/Consulting Active**: Won and currently engaged.
- **Closed Lost**: Disqualified or timing not right.

## 3. Weekly CRM Rhythm
- **Monday**: Review all leads in `Pipeline`. Update statuses and execute follow-ups.
- **Wednesday**: Post-Roundtable triage. Move new attendees into `Lead Captured`.
- **Friday**: Finance handoff for any `Proposal Sent` leads that converted.
